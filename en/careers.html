Careers at Tokyo Kanzai

Years of industry experience
and the skill to use AI

At Tokyo Kanzai, veterans with 75 years of experience behind them and younger staff who make full use of AI design "the next stage" on the same team. A rare environment where the stability of a long-established company and the challenge of a tech company exist side by side. A trading company dealing in iron where you can also take on AI development. Environments like that are hard to find.

WHY TOKYO KANZAI

Five Reasons

We have set out what it means to work at Tokyo Kanzai in five points. We hope some of them overlap with what matters to you.

01

75 years
in the industry

Founded in 1951. A solid track record and trust in the construction and industrial machinery industries become the foundation of your work.

02

Manufacturing ×
the AI frontier

We build in-house apps with generative AI, and we build the systems for using AI safely ourselves too. Efforts to use the apps count in performance reviews based on whether you took them on, not on how well they went (how TK Lab works).

03

A compact team
underpinned by AI

27 people handling JPY 2.9 billion in annual revenue. Each person has a clear role and real discretion, and uses AI to raise the strength of the whole organization.

04

Working with
the world

Through our Hong Kong subsidiary, we work with suppliers across Asia. International work is part of the stage.

05

A culture of
supporting each other

We remove dependence on individuals and leave to AI what AI can do. That is to build an organization where everyone can cover when someone is away on parental leave or for a life event. Beyond efficiency lies consideration for colleagues.

OPEN POSITIONS

Open Positions

If you have a specialty in any of the areas below, or are "young and have grit," we would like to hear from you at any time.

Sales, Metal Components

// SALES — METAL COMPONENTS

Sales of metal components for construction and industrial machinery. Proposals to customers, negotiations with suppliers, delivery management. The core of sales at a trading company.

→

Quality Assurance

// QUALITY ASSURANCE

A Quality Assurance Office of our own, unusual for a trading company. Inspection guidance for overseas suppliers, independent inspection at our logistics site, and response to quality defects. The job of making "no defective part crosses the sea" a reality.

→

International Business

// INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS

Working with our Hong Kong subsidiary, you develop suppliers in Asia, build relationships and shape sourcing strategy. A global role that includes multi-currency transactions and foreign-exchange decisions.

→

AI / Software Engineer

// AI / SOFTWARE ENGINEER — TK LAB CORE

You join TK Lab, the team that maintains the core business system while building in-house apps with generative AI. From the core business system to the cloud, RPA and web app development, you support the IT of a trading company across the board. You build tools for the floor together with the employees who use them.

→

Young and with Grit

// YOUNG & PASSIONATE

No industry experience required. What we value is the desire to try and to grow. Placement is decided together based on aptitude, and job rotation is possible.

WORKING CONDITIONS

Working Conditions

An outline of location, holidays and standard terms. Specific terms are decided in discussion during interviews.

Location Location
Tokyo head office (Uchisaiwaicho, Chiyoda-ku) / Atsugi Logistics Center (Atsugi, Kanagawa) / Hong Kong subsidiary
* Depends on position and assignment
Hours Hours
Standard working hours: 8 hours per day
Start and finish times vary by position (details provided at interview)
Holidays Holidays
Two days off every week (Saturday and Sunday) / Public holidays / Year-end and New Year holidays / Summer holidays / Annual paid leave / Other special leave
Compensation Compensation
Determined under company rules, taking experience, ability and age into account
Salary review: once a year / Bonus: twice a year (depending on results)
Benefits Benefits
Full social insurance (health insurance, employees' pension, employment insurance, workers' compensation) / Commuting allowance (within company rules) / Retirement allowance plan / Health checkups / Leave for family occasions, and more
Life Support Allowance Life Support Allowance
Our own allowance system to support each employee's quality of life. Employees raising children receive a "child allowance," and employees not raising children receive a "health promotion allowance," so that every employee can equally work toward a fuller life.
Type Type
Permanent employee (with probationary period)
* Mid-career and new graduate applications accepted at any time
SELECTION FLOW

Selection Process

Four steps. From application to offer typically takes about two to four weeks.

// STEP 01

Apply

Apply by email using the button below. Include your name, contact details, the position you are applying for and a brief statement of motivation, and attach your resume / CV (PDF).

→
// STEP 02

Document screening

We review your documents and invite you to an interview.
We reply within one week.

→
// STEP 03

Interviews (several)

Interviews at our office or online with the head of the department you would join and with our president. Two or three rounds depending on the position. They are also a chance for you to get to know us.

→
// STEP 04

Offer

After discussing terms, we set your start date. If you have any concerns about terms, ask us anything during the interviews.
