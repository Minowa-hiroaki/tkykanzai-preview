How We Handle Information

Information Policy

Information entrusted to us by customers and suppliers, and personal information received through inquiries and job applications, is handled under the following policy. Business information is masked before it goes into AI. That is our basic rule.

AI & BUSINESS INFORMATION

AI and Business Information

Precisely because we use generative AI in our work, we have built and operate our own system so that business information entrusted to us by customers and suppliers does not leave the company as-is.

Principle Principle
Business information (part numbers, order numbers, unit prices, amounts, quantities, due dates and the like) and information about people (addresses, salaries, health, IDs, passwords and the like) is not entered as-is into AI or external services (quantities and due dates may remain only within a copy in which company names and part numbers have been masked). This rule is set out in our "Rules for Using AI" for employees, which are distributed to all staff.
Masking before AI Masking
Our in-house app "AI Pre-Input Check" creates a masked copy with company names, part numbers, order numbers, unit prices and similar details hidden. When employees bring work documents to AI, they use the copy masked by this app. When the content of a document is to be entered without using the app, the employee consults internally first.
No-training setting No Training
The generative AI we use for work is set so that inputs are not used to train the model.
Record of what was sent Logging
When an in-house app sends data to AI, we keep a record of who sent it, when, and how much. The content sent is not recorded.
In-house apps In-House Apps
TK Lab's in-house apps run on PCs inside the company. Data is sent to outside companies only in the three cases listed under "Handling of Purchase Orders and Business Cards" below: purchase orders passed to the contracted DX-OCR provider, copies masked by AI Pre-Input Check, and images of business cards.
Informing employees Education
The rules are announced to all staff each time they are revised. The rules are: if in doubt, ask before entering anything; if something has gone out, report it at once; and the person who reports is never blamed.
ORDERS & BUSINESS CARDS

Handling of Purchase Orders and Business Cards

Purchase orders Purchase Orders
As a rule, purchase orders are read by our in-house reading app, only on PCs inside the company. The only external service that receives purchase orders is the DX-OCR provider we have a contract with. We are currently trialing the best approach, combining the two.
Requests to AI AI Requests
When an employee sends a request from "AI Pre-Input Check" to generative AI (OpenAI), only the masked copy is sent. The AI's answer is restored to the original values inside the app. The original values are not sent.
Business cards Business Cards
To read the text on business cards we receive, the image of the card is sent to an external AI service (OpenAI). Only the image of the card is sent, in a manner in which it is not used for training. It is retained by OpenAI for up to 30 days to check for misuse.
PRIVACY

Handling of Personal Information

Purpose of use Purpose
Information received through inquiries is used to respond and to contact you. Information received through job applications is used for selection and to contact you. Information on business cards is used for business contact.
Disclosure to third parties Disclosure
Except where required by law, we do not provide personal information to third parties without the person's consent. Companies entrusted with reading business cards or purchase orders receive information within the scope of that purpose. Such companies are selected and managed at the company level.
Management Management
Personal information we receive is handled only by those within the company who need it. Storage locations for paper and data are designated, and access is limited. Information no longer needed is disposed of appropriately.
Requests for disclosure Requests
Requests from the person concerned for disclosure, correction, suspension of use and the like are handled at the contact point below, after confirming the person's identity.
