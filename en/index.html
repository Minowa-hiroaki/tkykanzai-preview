MESSAGE FROM CEO

Ancient iron × cutting-edge AI

Iron is an ancient material that has supported human life for more than three thousand years. As a trading company dealing in iron, we have supported the lives of our customers, our suppliers, and our employees and their families for seventy-five years. Iron will remain at the center of Tokyo Kanzai's business.

Iron stays at the center of Tokyo Kanzai, but the way we do business will change. How we sell, how we prepare quotations, how we watch over quality, how we communicate with suppliers. We are bringing cutting-edge AI into every part of our work, and we will change in order to become more efficient and to create more value for our customers.

The purpose lies beyond efficiency. Even with fewer people, never to add to the burden on the colleagues we have now. To be an organization where, when someone takes parental leave or needs time with family, everyone covers for them and supports them. We remove dependence on individuals and leave to AI what AI can do. That is a decision made to protect the lives of the people we work with, and their families.

Handling ancient iron with cutting-edge AI. That is the next generation of Tokyo Kanzai.

// DATA SECURITY FIRST

Because we use AI,
information handling comes first

Business information entrusted to us by customers and suppliers is never entered into AI as-is. Before anything goes into AI, it is masked by an app we built in-house. This rule is built into a system that every employee uses.

// 01 MASKING

Masked by our app
before it reaches AI

Our in-house app "AI Pre-Input Check" creates a masked copy with company names, part numbers, order numbers, unit prices and similar details hidden. Employees use this app whenever they bring work documents to AI.

// 02 POLICY

Business information
stays inside

Our internal rules state that information that could identify a transaction is not entered as-is into AI or external services. The only outside party that receives purchase orders is the DX-OCR provider we have a contract with.

// 03 SETTING

No-training setting,
a record of what was sent

The generative AI we use for work is set so that inputs are not used for training. When an app sends data to AI, we log who sent it, when, and how much (the content itself is not kept).

// AFTER — the masked copy that goes into AI

【社名1】 様から、注番 【番号1】 で、品番 【番号2】 のフランジを 【数量1】、納期 【日付1】 でいただいています。単価は 【金額1】 です。担当の【名前1】様（【電話1】）に、納期のお返事を書きます。

A fictional text run through our actual "AI Pre-Input Check" app (the app works in Japanese). Company names, order and part numbers, quantities, dates, prices, names and phone numbers are replaced with tags such as 【社名1】 (company 1). Only the copy on the right goes into AI. The app also adds instructions for the AI, such as "use the tags exactly as they are", and restores the original values in the AI's answer inside the app.

QUALITY ASSURANCE / OUR 3 PILLARS

The 3 Pillars of Quality Assurance

Independent inspection at our own site, thorough root-cause analysis when defects occur, and the use of AI. Three pillars that keep defects from reaching our customers.

01

An established inspection system

Regular guidance for overseas suppliers, and our own inspection at our logistics site. Two lines of defense against defects getting out.

02

Response to quality defects

Tallying defect rates, checking the actual parts, analyzing root causes. With advice from staff who have worked in foundries, we work to resolve the underlying causes of defects.

03

Activities using AI

We are bringing AI into quality assurance as well. Digitizing inspection records and putting accumulated technical know-how into AI: we have begun combining human experience with AI.
