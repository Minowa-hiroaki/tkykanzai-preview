HISTORY

History

75 years since our founding in 1951. The business started by Kazuo Minowa is being carried into the next era by the sixth generation. Here is our story.

1951June
Founded
Founded by Kazuo Minowa as a sales company for pneumatic pipe fittings for Japanese National Railways rolling stock, manufactured by the former Jidosha Imono Co., Ltd. (later I Metal Technology, now IJTT).
The main product at founding was pipe fittings. Both the company name "Tokyo Kanzai" and the symbol mark we still use today reflect that original business. The business has since grown into a specialist trading company for metal components, but the symbol has never changed and continues to tell of where we began.
2007February
ECO STAGE Ⅰ certification
Obtained ECO STAGE Ⅰ environmental management system certification (February 14, 2007). As a small and medium-sized company, we made care for the global environment a pillar of management.
2007
Atsugi Logistics Center opened
Opened our own warehouse in Atsugi, Kanagawa. Set up repacking onto customer pallets and an inspection system, strengthening logistics and quality control.
2012
Hong Kong subsidiary established
Launched our overseas business brand TK International and established TK International Hong Kong Ltd. in Hong Kong. It operates as a sourcing base for foreign-currency transactions, handling imports from across Asia.
2017
Fourth president Tadateru Minowa retires
Led the company for many years and built its foundation of trust in the industry.
2020
Fifth president Yukiteru Minowa retires; Hiroaki Minowa becomes sixth president
Hiroaki Minowa of the founding Minowa family took office as the sixth president. Carrying on the spirit of the founding, he began management reforms for a new era.
2026
TK Lab launched / AI Pre-Input Check rolled out company-wide
Launched TK Lab, our in-house system development initiative. In-house business systems built with generative AI went into operation. Our in-house app AI Pre-Input Check, which masks information before employees enter it into AI, was made available to all employees. Purchase order reading is in trial, combining an outside partner's DX-OCR with an in-house app.
